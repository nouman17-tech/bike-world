Frequently Asked Questions

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery (COD), bank transfers, and online payment methods including JazzCash and EasyPaisa.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unused products in original packaging. Please contact our support team for return requests.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within Karachi. Delivery to other cities may take 5-7 business days.

Are your products genuine?

Yes, 100% of our products are genuine and sourced directly from authorized distributors and manufacturers.

Do you provide warranty?

Most products come with manufacturer warranty. Please check individual product pages for specific warranty details.

Can I track my order?

Yes, we provide order tracking. You will receive a tracking number via email once your order is shipped.
